1. About this policy

This policy covers the Global Islam mobile application, Android package com.globalislam.app, developed and operated by an individual using the public Google Play developer name Mahmoud_Jaber.craft. Global Islam is not operated through a registered company. For privacy questions, contact craftedbymahmoud@gmail.com. It explains information handled by the app and the external services it contacts.

The intended audience is adults aged 18 and older only. The individual operator is responsible for the application and its backend service; this public developer name is not presented as a legal personal name.

2. Information processed

  • Questions and searches: submitted AI and Hadith questions, selected categories, language, and retrieved source passages. Your text may contain personal information if you include it.
  • Voice input: microphone audio handled by your device's speech recognition service when you choose voice input; recognized text goes into the question field.
  • Location: foreground coordinates or manually entered coordinates and time zone. The saved operating-system location object may also include accuracy, altitude, heading, speed and a timestamp.
  • Device preferences: language, theme, prayer alerts, Adhan reciter, reading position, reader settings and Athkar progress.
  • Age eligibility: the app checks age in full years locally and saves an eligibility decision, including a blocked decision, rather than the age. For protected AI searches, it sends an adult declaration and policy version to obtain a short-lived server authorization. The server stores a digest of that authorization and its issue/expiry information.
  • Content reports: the selected AI answer, report category and language, and the question only if you choose to include it. A report receipt identifies your submission.
  • Requests and inquiries: services receive network information such as IP addresses and requested content identifiers. Email inquiries include your email address and message.

The app does not provide consumer account registration or profiles. No advertising SDK, configured Firebase Analytics or crash-reporting integration is used in the application code. Android notification dependencies include Firebase components; release configuration disables automatic FCM registration and Analytics collection, and the app does not register a remote push token. Local prayer alerts do not require remote push registration. These configuration statements do not guarantee the behavior of every operating-system or third-party component.

3. How information is used

The app uses information to answer submitted questions, search and translate religious content, retrieve prayer calendars, calculate the Qibla direction, deliver local prayer alerts, display lessons and news, download Adhan audio, and restore your preferences and reading progress.

Quran chapter filtering, lesson filtering, and comparison-question filtering run on the device. The Hadith search and AI submission features send their submitted text to online services.

4. AI assistant and voice input

Current service limitation: AI inference is temporarily unavailable because the hosting service cannot connect to the AI provider. Report submission and authorized review are available. We do not describe a failed request as successful AI processing.

Submitted questions and selected language go to the Global Islam backend hosted by Hugging Face Spaces. The backend uses Cloudflare Workers AI with Mistral Small 3.1 to check input and output safety, translate for retrieval where needed, and generate an explanation using retrieved religious material. This sends the current question, relevant source text and generated text to Cloudflare for those operations. The app does not attach earlier conversation turns or reading progress.

To reach Cloudflare, the backend may use a restricted relay hosted by Render. When used, the relay processes AI requests and responses in transit and forwards already-encrypted backup and deletion-journal objects. It does not receive their decryption keys or Neon database credentials. The relay code does not write request or response bodies to files, a database or application logs. Render may process network and service metadata under its own terms. Free hosting can sleep, delaying or preventing requests; an unavailable relay prevents AI processing rather than selecting another provider.

Model-generated explanations are labelled separately from retrieved excerpts. Exact quotation and source checks reduce fabricated references, but cannot guarantee religious or factual correctness. Do not treat AI answers as authoritative rulings or personalized medical, legal or other professional advice. Unsafe requests or unavailable safety checks can prevent an answer.

We do not create a persistent conversation-history database. Questions and answers remain in screen memory while the feature is used; reports you explicitly submit are stored separately. Ordinary diagnostic logs contain time and response status, not question/answer bodies. Hosting and AI providers also process service and network information under their own terms.

Earlier backend versions used Google Gemini and wrote submitted AI/Hadith text to hosting output. This update does not claim that earlier provider or hosting records have been erased. Clearing device data does not remove those earlier records, which remain subject to the relevant provider practices. The current backend has no Gemini fallback.

Cloudflare states that it does not use Customer Content to train models or improve Cloudflare or third-party services without explicit consent, and does not make that content available to other customers. See Cloudflare Workers AI data usage. This describes Cloudflare's commitment, not a promise that every infrastructure record is immediately erased. Mistral supplies the model; our application calls Cloudflare, rather than Mistral's direct API.

The answer screen lets you report offensive or unsafe AI content. Sending is explicit, the question is optional, and a receipt appears only after storage succeeds. The individual operator personally reviews reports through authenticated, authorized administrator access. Report content is encrypted before database storage.

Voice recognition may send audio to the device's speech-service provider; on-device-only recognition is not enforced. The application backend receives recognized text when you submit it, rather than a microphone recording. Use typed input if you prefer. Avoid including passwords, sensitive personal details or another person's private information.

5. Location, prayer times, and Qibla

The app requests foreground location access when you choose to obtain a location. You can enter coordinates and a time zone manually instead. A saved location is reused until you change it; the inspected app does not implement continuous background location tracking.

To retrieve prayer calendars, the app sends the saved latitude and longitude, calendar month/year, calculation settings, and, when available, time zone to AlAdhan over HTTPS. Manually entered coordinates are also sent. Permission denial does not remove a previously saved location or prevent its use for prayer requests.

Qibla bearings and live compass headings are processed on the device. The heading subscription stops when the app moves to the background. Qibla uses the same saved location as prayer times.

6. Local device storage

The app uses AsyncStorage for saved location, language and theme, prayer preferences and cached calendars, reading position, Quran reader settings, Athkar progress, and cached content, translations, and news. Downloaded Adhan files are stored in the app’s document directory. Native prayer schedules and recovery information are stored locally for alarms and reboot recovery.

The app does not implement an additional encryption layer for these records. Android backup is enabled in the current app configuration, so operating-system backup or restore may retain or restore some app data according to your device settings. Local storage is therefore not a promise that no backup copy can exist.

7. Accounts and authentication

The inspected version does not provide account creation, sign-in, or user profiles. You do not need a Global Islam account to use it. There is no in-app account deletion feature because no app account system is implemented.

8. Service providers and transfers

  • Hugging Face Spaces: hosts the Global Islam backend handling AI/Hadith submissions, reports and content requests.
  • Render: hosts the restricted, authenticated Cloudflare relay. Religious retrieval indexes, the embedding model, database access and recovery decryption keys remain on Hugging Face. The relay handles AI text in transit and encrypted recovery objects, with separate provider infrastructure practices.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI: processes questions, retrieved source material, translations and generated output for inference and safety checks. Cloudflare KV stores encrypted database backups and deletion-journal objects.
  • Neon PostgreSQL: stores backend records, short-lived authorization digests, encrypted reports and administrative review metadata. We do not claim a user-selected storage country.
  • Google translation endpoint: receives content and language pairs when translating displayed lessons, comparisons, news or Hadith content and, in older client paths, submitted non-English search text.
  • AlAdhan: receives coordinates and prayer-calendar parameters over HTTPS.
  • HadeethEnc and GitHub-hosted Hadith content: receive requested content, language and collection identifiers.
  • Quranpedia: receives chapter, verse and language identifiers for Quran translations.
  • Quran Tafseer API: receives commentary source, chapter and verse requests. Its current ten-source integration uses HTTP; see the security section.
  • Al Jazeera, audio and image hosts: receive news-feed, Adhan-file and lesson-thumbnail requests. External destinations depend on the selected content.
  • Device speech services and Android service components: support voice recognition and notifications as described above.
  • Email services: process privacy correspondence sent to our contact address.

These services receive the information needed for requests and network metadata, including IP addresses. Opening external news, videos, lessons or source links subjects the visit to that destination's practices. Providers may process information in countries other than yours. Their infrastructure logs, legal obligations and independent processing are governed by their own terms; our application cleanup does not control every provider record.

This policy website uses GitHub Pages. Its files contain no analytics scripts, forms, cookies or external fonts. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security; see the GitHub privacy statement. Only an opaque recovery digest, not a database, report, deletion-journal content or key, is recorded in the application's GitHub repository to verify recovery checkpoints.

9. Security

Backend requests and its PostgreSQL and Cloudflare connections use TLS with certificate validation. Reports are encrypted before database storage; backups and deletion-journal objects are separately encrypted. Administrative report access requires authorization. These safeguards do not make a device, provider or service immune to compromise.

The existing ten-source Quran Tafseer integration still uses unencrypted HTTP. Commentary identifiers and network metadata on that connection can be observed, and content could be altered in transit. Other explicitly configured API and audio requests use HTTPS; some image and external-link destinations depend on content. We therefore do not claim all app traffic is encrypted. Local app preferences have no additional app-level encryption layer.

10. Retention and deletion

Saved preferences, location, progress, cached content and downloaded audio remain on your device until replaced or removed with app data. There is no general expiry or single in-app delete-all control. Changing location does not necessarily erase older calendar cache entries.

On Android, use device Settings → Apps → Global Islam → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage/data. This resets local preferences, saved location, progress, caches, downloaded Adhan and alarm schedules. Reconfigure prayer alerts afterward. Uninstalling normally removes local app data; manage operating-system backup copies separately. Clearing device data does not erase prior online submissions.

  • Application diagnostic logs: the approved maximum is 30 days; they record time and response status, rather than AI content. Ephemeral hosting storage may remove them earlier.
  • AI reports: the approved rule requires review within 30 days and deletion 90 days after resolution, or a maximum of 180 days for unresolved reports, unless a documented, limited lawful necessity applies. Report access is limited by these dates; physical deletion requires the recovery-protected maintenance procedure.
  • Application-managed database backups: an encrypted production snapshot has been created and its integrity checked. Stored backup objects expire after 30 days. Daily backup automation is prepared but is not yet active. An independently checkpointed encrypted deletion journal is used to reapply completed deletions before restored data is returned to service.
  • Adult authorizations: expire after five minutes. Expired authorization records and administrative audit metadata are cleaned by scheduled maintenance; audit metadata is retained for 30 days.

The hourly maintenance workflow is prepared but disabled while a host-to-storage connectivity failure is resolved. Automatic report purging, daily backup creation and log cleanup are therefore not yet operational. The approved periods above must not be read as a claim that automatic enforcement has succeeded. The operator remains responsible for handling privacy requests and completing verified maintenance. The recovery procedure has been tested in an isolated database, not by restoring production data. Recovery verification must succeed before physical report deletion; a failed job is an operational incident requiring correction, not proof of completed deletion. Lawful exceptions must be documented and limited to necessity. Provider-controlled infrastructure logs, recovery history and system backups may follow separate provider schedules; the application-managed 30-day backup rule does not describe every provider copy.

To request access or deletion, email craftedbymahmoud@gmail.com. Include your report receipt where relevant, without sending passwords or identity documents. The individual operator handles requests within 30 days or sooner where law requires, may proportionately verify the request, and explains any lawful limit. Ordinary AI conversations are not persistently indexed for retrieval/deletion by user identity. We do not promise to locate data that cannot reasonably be identified, or to erase records outside our control instantly.

11. Adults-only eligibility and minors

Global Islam is intended for adults aged 18 and older only. It is not designed or intentionally offered to children or teenagers. The app asks for age in full years before loading feature providers, blocks declared minors and retains the blocked decision across restart. Previous younger-audience approvals are not accepted.

Protected backend AI endpoints require a current, short-lived server-issued authorization based on an adult declaration. A client eligibility boolean alone does not authorize a request. No birth date, government ID or identity document is requested. Self-declaration is not proof of adulthood: a false declaration, cleared app data or another device can defeat the age assertion. Store age restrictions also do not prove a person's age.

If you believe a person below 18 has submitted personal information, contact craftedbymahmoud@gmail.com so the operator can investigate and handle the information appropriately. We do not claim automatic age detection or that information about minors can never reach an external service.

12. Your rights and choices

You can decline or revoke microphone and location access in device settings, type questions, enter coordinates manually, change your saved location, disable prayer alerts, avoid optional AI and external links, and clear local storage. Permission revocation does not erase saved data or earlier transmissions. You choose whether to include a question in an AI report.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information and complain to a privacy authority. Contact craftedbymahmoud@gmail.com. The individual operator is responsible for handling these requests within the period described above; email is handled manually, not by an automated deletion promise.
