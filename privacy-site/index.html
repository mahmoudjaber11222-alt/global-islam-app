1. About this policy

This policy covers the Global Islam mobile application, Android package com.globalislam.app, developed by Mahmoud_Jaber.craft. For privacy questions, contact craftedbymahmoud@gmail.com. It explains information handled by the app and the external services it contacts.

The intended audience is people aged 14 and older, including teenagers and adults. The app’s association with theislam.us does not establish the production backend operator.

2. Information processed

  • Questions and searches: text you submit to the AI assistant and Hadith search, selected categories, and the language used. Your text may contain personal information if you include it.
  • Voice input: microphone audio processed by your device’s speech recognition service when you choose voice input. The resulting text is placed in the question field.
  • Location: coordinates obtained with foreground location permission or coordinates and a time zone you enter manually. The saved device location object may also contain accuracy, altitude, heading, speed, and a timestamp when supplied by the operating system.
  • Selections and preferences: language, theme, prayer alert settings, selected Adhan reciter, reading position and reader preferences, and Athkar counts.
  • Online requests: requested Hadith or Quran passage identifiers, translation languages, content and media requests. The receiving service necessarily receives your network IP address and request information; its logging practices require confirmation.
  • Privacy inquiries: if you email the privacy contact, your sender address and message content are processed through email. Avoid including unnecessary sensitive details, especially information about a minor.
  • Age eligibility: an age entered in full years is checked on the device. A valid submission is cleared from the field; only an eligibility flag is saved. The age is not sent to the app’s online services.

The inspected app has no account registration, profile forms, advertising integration, or configured analytics or crash reporting service. Android dependencies include Firebase Cloud Messaging through Expo notifications. No app push-token registration or Firebase project configuration was found. Current Android source disables automatic FCM registration and Analytics collection defaults; this does not establish that every SDK performs no collection. The final binary and runtime behavior still require verification. This does not establish the practices of remote services.

3. How information is used

The app uses information to answer submitted questions, search and translate religious content, retrieve prayer calendars, calculate the Qibla direction, deliver local prayer alerts, display lessons and news, download Adhan audio, and restore your preferences and reading progress.

Quran chapter filtering, lesson filtering, and comparison-question filtering run on the device. The Hadith search and AI submission features send their submitted text to online services.

4. AI assistant and voice input

When you submit an AI question, the app sends the question and selected language to a Global Islam backend hosted on Hugging Face Spaces. For a language other than English, it first sends the question and source/target language to Google’s translation endpoint. If translation fails, the original text can still be sent to the backend.

Each AI request contains the current question; the inspected app does not attach earlier turns, an account ID, or saved reading progress. The receiving service can see your network IP address.

The app keeps the question and answer in screen memory; it does not implement persistent chat history. When using voice input, the device’s speech service may process audio remotely. On-device-only recognition is not required by the app. The recognized text is sent for AI processing only when you submit it.

Backend source matching the host’s reported deployed revision uses Google’s Gemini API to process the question together with retrieved religious source text, and writes AI and Hadith request bodies and questions to server output. A separate verse-translation handler requests translations from Quranpedia using chapter, verse and language identifiers.

Release blocked: the inspected Gemini API terms do not support this intended 14+ audience. The backend operator, applicable provider contract and service tier, logging retention, deletion, processing locations, training/data-use settings, and moderation/reporting must be verified or resolved before publication. No promise of no training or no server retention is made. Speech-service practices depend on the device.

The answer screen includes a reporting control. Its receiving service is not yet configured or verified, so no report can currently be submitted. The prepared flow requires an explicit send action; the question is attached only if you choose to include it. Actual report storage, review and retention must be established before enabling this flow.

Avoid including sensitive personal details in questions. You can use typed input instead of the microphone and choose whether to use the AI assistant.

5. Location, prayer times, and Qibla

The app requests foreground location access when you choose to obtain a location. You can enter coordinates and a time zone manually instead. A saved location is reused until you change it; the inspected app does not implement continuous background location tracking.

To retrieve prayer calendars, the app sends the saved latitude and longitude, calendar month/year, calculation settings, and, when available, time zone to AlAdhan over HTTPS. Manually entered coordinates are also sent. Permission denial does not remove a previously saved location or prevent its use for prayer requests.

Qibla bearings and live compass headings are processed on the device. The heading subscription stops when the app moves to the background. Qibla uses the same saved location as prayer times.

6. Local device storage

The app uses AsyncStorage for saved location, language and theme, prayer preferences and cached calendars, reading position, Quran reader settings, Athkar progress, and cached content, translations, and news. Downloaded Adhan files are stored in the app’s document directory. Native prayer schedules and recovery information are stored locally for alarms and reboot recovery.

The app does not implement an additional encryption layer for these records. Android backup is enabled in the current app configuration, so operating-system backup or restore may retain or restore some app data according to your device settings. Local storage is therefore not a promise that no backup copy can exist.

7. Accounts and authentication

The inspected version does not provide account creation, sign-in, or user profiles. You do not need a Global Islam account to use it. There is no in-app account deletion feature because no app account system is implemented.

8. External services and data transfers

Depending on the features you use, requests go to:

  • Hugging Face Spaces-hosted Global Islam backend: AI questions, Hadith queries/categories and language, Quran passage translation requests, lessons, and comparison content. The inspected public backend source calls Google Gemini for AI answers and Quranpedia for verse translations; hosting metadata reports this source revision as running; provider account and operating practices still require verification.
  • Google translation endpoint: non-English AI and Hadith input requiring translation, and content translated for display, such as lesson, comparison, and news text.
  • AlAdhan: coordinates and prayer calendar parameters.
  • HadeethEnc and GitHub-hosted Hadith content: requested Hadith, language, and collection identifiers.
  • Quran Tafseer API: requested commentary source, chapter, and verse, using HTTP in the current version.
  • Al Jazeera: the news feed requested by the app’s news ticker.
  • GitHub-hosted audio and lesson image hosts: requested Adhan files and lesson thumbnails. Image destinations depend on the lesson content.
  • Your device’s speech recognition service: voice recognition, which may use remote processing.
  • Bundled Android service components: Expo notifications includes Google Firebase Cloud Messaging; an existing release manifest also includes Firebase Installations and Google ML Kit components. Their presence does not establish which data is transmitted. Runtime initialization and collection remain unverified.

These services receive network request information, including IP addresses. Opening a lesson, video, news story, AI resource, or other external link takes you to the destination service, where its own privacy practices apply.

This policy website’s files contain no analytics scripts, forms, cookies, or external font requests. The website has not yet been published. GitHub Pages is the planned host; GitHub documents that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub privacy statement. Actual hosting must be verified before publication.

Pending confirmation: server-side subprocessors, provider contracts and independent uses, transfers between countries, server-side advertising/analytics, and provider retention. No claim about selling data, training, or restricted provider use is made without verification.

9. Security

Most explicitly configured API and audio requests use HTTPS. The current Quran Tafseer request uses unencrypted HTTP, and Android permits cleartext traffic. Lesson image and external-link destinations also depend on content. A claim that every transmission is encrypted would be inaccurate for this version.

No system can guarantee complete security. Backend access controls, encryption at rest, monitoring, and other server security practices remain unverified.

10. Retention and deletion

Saved preferences, location, progress, cached content, and downloaded audio remain on the device until replaced or removed with app data. The inspected implementation does not apply a general expiry period or offer a single “delete all data” control. Changing location does not necessarily remove older calendar cache entries.

On Android, open device Settings → Apps → Global Islam → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage/data to remove local app data. Labels vary by device. This resets settings, saved location, reading and Athkar progress, cached content, downloaded Adhan files, and local alarm schedules; prayer alerts must be configured again. Manage system backup copies separately. Uninstalling normally removes the app’s local data, but backup/restore behavior is controlled by the operating system.

Clearing device data does not delete information already sent to online services. The app has no verified server-side deletion request mechanism.

Pending confirmation: server and provider retention periods or criteria, backups, logs, legal exceptions, and a usable process for privacy/deletion requests. These must be resolved with the confirmed operator before publication.

11. Children’s and teenagers’ privacy

Global Islam is intended for people aged 14 and older. It is not intended for children younger than 14. Teenagers remain minors in many places, and applicable protections and consent requirements can differ by country.

Before app feature providers load, the app asks for age in full years unless this device already has a saved eligibility flag. An answer below 14 is denied access. Only the eligibility flag is saved; the age itself is not saved or transmitted. This is a self-declared check, not identity verification, parental consent or an assurance about the age of every person sharing a device. It does not establish that native SDKs perform no processing before the check.

Once access is allowed, online features do not distinguish teenagers from adults: submitted AI/Hadith text, selected coordinates and requests can leave the device as described above. Voice input may involve remote speech recognition.

Do not include names, addresses, school details, contact information or other sensitive personal details in questions. Parents or guardians can contact craftedbymahmoud@gmail.com to raise concerns or ask about a minor’s information. A privacy email is not evidence that remote deletion has been implemented.

Pending verification: compatible provider terms for teenagers, backend moderation/reporting, native SDK startup, any required parental consent, and a process to address information concerning users below the intended age. The age check does not resolve the current Gemini API age restriction. No claim is made that the service collects no children’s data, automatically deletes it, or is certified for every country.

12. Your rights and choices

You can decline or revoke microphone and location permissions in device settings, use typed input or manually entered coordinates, change your saved location, disable prayer alerts in Settings, avoid optional AI submissions and external links, and clear local app storage. Revoking a permission does not erase stored data or earlier service requests.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information and to raise a complaint with a privacy authority. You or your parent or guardian can contact craftedbymahmoud@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Pending verification: the operational process for handling requests, identity checks, remote access/deletion capabilities and retention of privacy correspondence. No unverified response time or server deletion guarantee is made.
